Internal Accounting App — effective October 5, 2026

Internal Accounting App ("the Application") is an internal software tool owned and operated by Sudstainables LLC and Seventh Coast Ventures LLC (each a "Company", together the "Companies"). It connects each Company's own QuickBooks Online company file to that Company's own accounting and reporting tools.

1. Private use only

The Application is used exclusively by the Companies. It is not distributed, licensed, sold, or made available to any third party, and no third party may use it.

2. Authorized users

Only the owners and authorized staff of a Company may connect that Company's QuickBooks Online company file to the Application. By connecting a company file you confirm you are authorized to grant the Application access to it.

3. Scope of access

The Application reads accounting data (reports, accounts, transactions and attachments) to maintain the Company's internal financial records and reports. When enabled by the Company, it also creates and updates accounting records in the Company's own file, such as transactions, journal entries, reconciliation adjustments and document attachments, as part of the Company's bookkeeping, under the oversight of the Company's authorized staff.

4. No warranty

The Application is provided "as is" for the Companies' internal use, without warranty of any kind.

5. Termination

Access may be revoked at any time by disconnecting the Application from within QuickBooks Online (Apps → My Apps) or by the Company deleting the stored authorization.
