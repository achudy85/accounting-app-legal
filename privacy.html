Privacy Policy

Internal Accounting App — effective October 5, 2026

This policy covers Internal Accounting App ("the Application"), an internal tool operated by Sudstainables LLC and Seventh Coast Ventures LLC (each a "Company", together the "Companies").

What the Application accesses

With a Company's authorization, the Application accesses that Company's own QuickBooks Online accounting data: accounts, financial reports, transaction records, related customer and vendor records, and document attachments.

How the data is used

The data is used only for the Company's own bookkeeping, financial reporting, reconciliation and financial planning.

Where data is stored

Authorization tokens are stored on a computer owned and physically controlled by the Companies, with access restricted to the Companies' own service account. A copy of the accounting data is kept in the Companies' private, access-controlled database, hosted by a cloud database provider under the Companies' own account, for internal reporting and analysis.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. The only recipients are the Companies' own owners, authorized staff and internal tools operating on the Companies' behalf, plus the service providers that host the Companies' own systems.

Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are kept only while a company file remains connected. Disconnecting the Application from QuickBooks Online (Apps → My Apps) revokes access, and the Company then deletes the stored token. Accounting records are retained as long as the Company needs them for its financial records.

Security

All traffic between the Application and Intuit uses HTTPS. Stored credentials and data are restricted to the Companies' authorized accounts.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the owners of the Companies.
